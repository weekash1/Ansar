Glasgow ANSAR is committed to creating a safe, inclusive environment for everyone who takes part in our sport, arts, leadership and community programmes. We take our responsibility to protect children, young people and adults at risk seriously, and we expect every member of staff, volunteer, coach and trustee to share that commitment.

Reporting a Concern

If you have a concern about the safety or wellbeing of a child, young person or adult at risk connected to Glasgow ANSAR, please contact our Designated Safeguarding Officer directly using the details above. If a child or adult is in immediate danger, always call 999.

You can also contact the following external organisations for advice or to report a concern:

  • Police Scotland (non-emergency) — 101
  • NSPCC Helpline — 0808 800 5000
  • Childline (for children and young people) — 0800 1111

Recruitment & Training

All staff and volunteers working directly with children, young people or adults at risk undergo a Protecting Vulnerable Groups (PVG) scheme check through Disclosure Scotland before starting their role, and receive safeguarding training appropriate to their position.

About Glasgow ANSAR

Empowering Communities Since 2001

ANSAR (All Nations Sports, Arts and Recreation) is a community-led charity based in Glasgow, dedicated to empowering people through sport, arts, recreation, and volunteering.

Who We Are

Creating Spaces Where People Belong and Thrive

We believe that everyone deserves the opportunity to participate, belong, and thrive. Through inclusive programmes and meaningful community engagement, we support children, young people, women, and families to build confidence, develop skills, improve wellbeing, and create lasting connections.

Our work brings people together from diverse backgrounds, creating safe and welcoming spaces where individuals can grow, lead, and reach their potential.

“Our community is strongest when everyone has the opportunity to shine.”

What We Do

Inspiring Positive Change Across Glasgow

From grassroots sport and creative arts to leadership development, volunteering, wellbeing initiatives, and community events, ANSAR provides opportunities that inspire positive change and strengthen communities.

Sport & Recreation
Inclusive activities that build confidence, teamwork, and wellbeing.
Arts & Creativity
Creative programmes that encourage expression, imagination, and skill-building.
Leadership & Volunteering
Developing role models and empowering people to lead in their communities.

Our Purpose

Our Vision & Our Mission

Vision: A community where everyone has the opportunity to reach their full potential.

Mission: To empower people through sport, arts, recreation, and community engagement — creating positive change for individuals, families, and communities.

“We believe in the power of community to transform lives.”

Our Values

These principles guide everything we do at ANSAR and shape the way we support our communities.

Inclusion

We welcome everyone and celebrate diversity.

Empowerment

Community

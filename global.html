Global Girls Fund

Empowering Girls. Creating Opportunities.

The Global Girls Fund was established to help girls and young women overcome barriers and reach their full potential — locally and globally.

Why We Exist

Changing Lives Through Opportunity & Empowerment

Around the world and within our own communities, many girls face challenges that limit their access to sport, education, leadership opportunities, travel, and personal development experiences.

We believe every girl deserves the chance to dream big, build confidence, and create her own future. Through the Global Girls Fund, we provide opportunities that inspire, empower, and equip girls to thrive.

“When you invest in a girl, you invest in a stronger future for everyone.”

What We Support

Opening Doors to New Possibilities

The fund helps girls and young women access:

  • Sport and physical activity programmes
  • Leadership and volunteering opportunities
  • Personal development and confidence-building activities
  • Educational workshops and training
  • Wellbeing and mental health initiatives
  • Cultural and international experiences
  • Equipment, travel, and participation costs

Why It Matters

Investing in Girls Transforms Communities

When girls are given opportunities, entire communities benefit. By investing in girls, we help build:

  • Confidence and self-belief
  • Leadership skills
  • Positive physical and mental wellbeing
  • Stronger communities
  • Future role models and changemakers
“Empowered girls become empowered women — and empowered women change the world.”

Our Programmes

Participation Transforms Lives

At ANSAR, we believe that participation has the power to transform lives. Our programmes provide opportunities for people of all ages and backgrounds to be active, creative, connected, and empowered.

What We Offer

Inclusive Programmes for All Ages & Backgrounds

Whether through sport, arts, volunteering, or wellbeing activities, we create inclusive spaces where individuals can develop skills, build confidence, and become part of a supportive community.

“Everyone deserves the opportunity to participate, belong, and thrive.”

Sport

Building Confidence Through Active Participation

Sport is at the heart of what we do. We offer a range of activities designed to increase participation, improve physical and mental wellbeing, and create pathways for personal development.

Activities include:
  • Football
  • Netball
  • Cycling
  • Cricket
  • Swimming
  • Multi-sport programmes
  • Holiday camps and festivals

Arts & Creativity

Inspiring Expression & Creative Growth

Our arts programmes provide opportunities for participants to express themselves, explore new ideas, and develop creative skills.

Activities include:
  • Theatre and performance
  • Film and media projects
  • Photography
  • Storytelling
  • Spoken word and creative writing
  • Community arts projects

Youth Leadership & Volunteering

Developing the Next Generation

We invest in the next generation of leaders by providing volunteering opportunities, training, mentoring, and practical experience.

Young people can:
  • Gain leadership skills
  • Achieve accredited awards
  • Support community events
  • Become coaches and mentors
  • Build confidence and employability skills

Women & Girls

Creating Safe & Empowering Spaces

We are committed to creating safe, welcoming, and empowering opportunities for women and girls.

Programmes include:
  • Sports sessions
  • Wellbeing retreats
  • Workshops and training
  • Leadership opportunities
  • Social and community events

Community & Wellbeing

Strengthening Connections

Strong communities are built through connection. Our wellbeing and community programmes help reduce isolation, improve confidence, and create opportunities for people to come together.

This includes:
  • Family activities
  • Community events
  • Health and wellbeing workshops
  • Outdoor activities
  • Social action projects
  • Cultural celebrations
